About

WiThemes is a WordPress theme studio, founded in 2013. We used to sell premium themes on ThemeForest, but in 2026 we’re moving away from it. There are two reasons:

  1. Envato is in decline and is no longer a good home for theme authors.
  2. We want to grow, so we’re distributing our themes on wordpress.org and here.

History

Here are some of our milestones:

2013 — we launched our first theme, Blank.

2015 — Fox, our flagship theme: a modern theme built for magazines and newspapers.

2019 — Dine, our last theme on ThemeForest.

2019–2026 — we focused on updating our existing ThemeForest themes.

June 2026 — Stoat, our first free theme, available to download on wordpress.org.

2026 onwards — we’ll build more themes. Some will be free on wordpress.org, and our existing ThemeForest themes will still be maintained.
