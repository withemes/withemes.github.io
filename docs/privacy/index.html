Privacy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

When you purchase a theme from us, LemonSqueezy collects your name, email address, and payment details on our behalf. We use your email to deliver your purchase, send updates, and provide support.

What we don’t do

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not track you across other websites. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.

Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic site functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

  • LemonSqueezy processes payments and manages licenses. Their privacy policy applies to payment transactions.
  • Analytics — we may use privacy-friendly analytics (no personal data collected).

Your rights

You can request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

Contact

If you have questions about these terms or your privacy, reach out via our contact page.
